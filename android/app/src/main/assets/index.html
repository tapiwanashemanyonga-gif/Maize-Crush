QUICK START

How to harvest

Connect crops; do not swap them.

  1. Touch and drag across matching neighbouring crops.
  2. Connect at least three; diagonals count.
  3. Five or six trigger Golden Harvest; seven or more trigger Harvest Storm.
  4. Hints are free. Rain Shower and Fertilizer each cost one move.
  5. Reach the goal before moves run out.
