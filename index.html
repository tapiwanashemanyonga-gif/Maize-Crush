QUICK START

How to harvest

Connect crops; do not swap them.

  1. Touch and drag across matching neighbouring crops.
  2. Connect at least three; diagonals count.
  3. Four crops trigger a nearby 3×3 Seed Burst; five or six trigger Golden Harvest; seven or more trigger Harvest Storm.
  4. Hints are free. Rain Shower, Fertilizer and Market Cart each cost one move.
  5. Complete the highlighted level objective before moves run out. Goals change from level to level: score targets, named-crop collection, long chains, cascades, special harvests, successful connections and using a farm booster strategically.
  6. Levels continue endlessly through Zimbabwe-inspired worlds. Your progress and objective progress are saved on this device.
